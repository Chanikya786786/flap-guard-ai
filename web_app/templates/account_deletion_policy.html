Account Deletion & {{ grace_days }}-Day Recovery Window

FlapGuard AI provides self-service account deletion on both Web and Android.

  1. Sign in to FlapGuard AI (Web or Android) and open Profile → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm with your password to schedule permanent deletion in {{ grace_days }} days (exact date and time displayed).
  3. All active sessions and mobile tokens are revoked immediately.
  4. Restore anytime before day {{ grace_days }}: Sign back in with your email and password before the deadline to cancel deletion and restore your account.
  5. What gets deleted vs. preserved: After {{ grace_days }} days, your name, email, profile photo, and password hash are permanently erased and anonymized. Clinical ward records and security audit logs remain anonymized for clinical and audit integrity.
